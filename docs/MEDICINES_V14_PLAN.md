# V14 plan: medicines come from one master list (Q1, written before any change)

Owner's wish (4 and 7 Nov 2026): first a **master list** of every medicine and vaccine used on the farm, then a link to **which flock gets what**, with the vaccination schedule built in and boosters tracked per flock. Entry is "pick from the list, tick the flocks", never typing a name.

**Live data:** `medicine_lines` has **0 rows**, `vaccination_schedule` **0 rows**, no "none this month" answers. Nothing can be lost, but the migration is still written to keep any line that gets entered before it runs (below). The login tables of migration 0048 (`login_attempts`, `auth_state`) stay in the database, unused; V14 numbers its migrations 0049 and up.

## What changes

1. **Master list.** New tables `medicine_catalogue` (one row per medicine, vaccine or management task, name as the owner writes it, unique on lower(name) per farm) and `medicine_aliases` (other spellings; an alias can never equal another item's name). Seeded from the owner's schedule: every name he wrote, with kind, purpose and route only where he stated them; **"guess" items are marked `needs_confirmation`** (shown as a "to confirm" badge) and nothing is invented (no doses, no brands that he did not give). Screen **Medicines list**: search, add, edit, deactivate (never delete an item that is used), mark as confirmed, and tick which booster an item counts as.
2. **Usage linked to the list.** `medicine_lines` gets `catalogue_id` (nullable), `entry_group` (lines saved together) and `cost_total_inr` / `quantity_total` (what was typed for the whole entry). The entry form **picks** from the list (search), has an inline "add a new medicine" that creates a list item marked "to confirm", then **ticks the flocks** (shortcuts: whole farm, main group, young group). **One line is written per flock**, so per-flock reports are exact. A total cost or quantity is **split by the number of birds in each flock on that date**; if a flock's bird count is not known the split is equal and the screen says so; the lines always add up to the total exactly (rounding goes to the biggest share). Month totals, "None this month", edit and delete with confirmation, "Save and add next" and the cost flow into Costs are kept unchanged.
3. **Old free-text lines** (none today): matched to the list when the name or an alias matches exactly (migration, and again whenever an item or alias is added); otherwise `catalogue_id` stays empty and they appear under **"Not matched to the list"** with a one-tap match for all lines with that name.
4. **Per flock.** A new screen **What is each flock on**, and the same card on every flock page: what was given (latest first), last ND booster and last LPAI booster, next due, with traffic lights (green done, amber due within 14 days or no date entered, red overdue). Mobile cards.
5. **Boosters every 6 months.** New table `vaccine_repeat_rules` (ND booster, LPAI booster: repeat every 6 months, all laying flocks) and `vaccine_repeat_rule_items` (which list items count towards a rule). Next due = last given + 6 calendar months (month ends are clamped: 31 Aug + 6 months = 28 Feb, 29 Feb 2028 + 6 months = 29 Aug 2028). **A flock with no recorded date shows "last date not entered" in amber with a one-tap "enter last date"; nothing is ever assumed and a missing date is never shown as done.** Today gets one grouped alert per vaccine (booster due within 14 days or overdue, listing the flocks), plus one line when dates are missing: no alert storms.
6. **Chick vaccination plan.** New table `vaccination_schedule_template` (day from, day to, list item, note) seeded with the owner's day-by-day schedule, and three nullable columns on `vaccination_schedule` (`catalogue_id`, `template_id`, `window_end`). Screen **Chick vaccination plan**: the schedule day by day, a **preview for a new batch** (any date, nothing saved), and **make the plan** for a batch (day 1 = arrival date): each step with its date, tick-off with the actual date, overdue in red; debeaking (day 9) and nail cutting (day 39) shown as tasks. A newly registered chick batch gets its plan automatically; **existing batches and all current flocks get nothing automatically** (the flocks are past day 105; batch 3 of 18 Sep is on day 51 today, and the owner presses "Make the plan" and ticks what was done, if he wants it).
7. **Import.** The V12 CSV/Excel import now takes the list name (or an alias), one or several flock labels (`BAB-1; BAB-2`, or `main`, `young`, `all`), date, quantity, cost, with a template and a dry run. Names the list does not know are listed for the owner to match or to add as "to confirm"; nothing is created silently.
8. **Plain words and checks.** `scripts/medicines-v14-check.mts` (uniqueness and aliases, one entry for several flocks with an exact split, the booster rule including "no date" and month-end cases, plan generation from a placement date, grouped alerts, matching of old lines, cost flow) added to `scripts/run-all.mts`.

## How old data is carried over

Additive only: new tables, new nullable columns, no row is changed except `catalogue_id` on lines whose name exactly matches. `kind`, `item`, `scope`, `flock_internal_id`, `shed_code`, cost and photo stay as they are, so the Costs screen and every old report read the same numbers. The old typed `item` stays on the line as what was written; for matched lines the screen shows the list name.

## Decisions taken (for the record, also in DECISIONS.md)

- A catalogue item is **never deleted once used**; it is deactivated (it then no longer appears in the pick list but old lines keep their name).
- Management tasks (debeaking, nail cutting) are list items of kind `management`; they appear only in the chick plan as tasks, not in the medicine entry form.
- "ND booster (lay)" and "LPAI booster (lay)" are created as list items marked to confirm, because the exact products are not known. Any other list item (for example LASOTA or the owner's real ND product) can be ticked as "counts as an ND booster" later.
- A line for the whole farm or a shed (V12 style) still counts towards a booster: whole farm for every laying flock, a shed for the flocks now in that shed. New entries always write one line per flock.
- Product choice "standard or B2K" for the day-23 IBD booster is one list item ("IBD booster (standard or B2K)") with the two spellings as aliases; the product used is written in the line's note.
